Field guide

Moving the chains

Gain 10 yards in four downs. A touchdown scores seven, including the extra point. Tap a play to snap the ball. On a pass, flick upfield toward a receiver, leading their route. On a run or after a catch, swipe sideways to cut. On defense, choose a coverage and swipe to steer a defender.

Distance pays

Choose a field goal on any down. The play card shows the kick distance, value and your fold’s maximum range. Flick straight upfield. Long attempts need more power and leave less room for error.

One field, two iPads

Open the game on both iPads on the same Wi-Fi. The host chooses the two teams and paper weight and plays as the home team. Choose Two iPads on both, then host on one and join on the other. Lay them flat with the tops of the game screens touching at midfield. For the closest fit, use matching screen sizes, lock the same orientation, and launch from the Home Screen to hide the browser toolbar.
